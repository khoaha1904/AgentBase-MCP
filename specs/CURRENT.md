# Current capability

Active capability: [`016-canonical-okf-graph`](016-canonical-okf-graph/spec.md).

Capability 016 makes OKF identity entity-centered rather than repository-tree
centered. One canonical concept may be supported by several repositories;
domains remain optional, repository concepts retain only source-specific
knowledge, and v5 evaluates useful owner-review structure without requiring a
complete inventory. Governed question persistence remains future scope.
