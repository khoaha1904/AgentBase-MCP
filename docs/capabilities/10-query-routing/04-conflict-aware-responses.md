# 10.04 — Conflict-aware responses

> Status: Host composition through Published search/read; no conflict scorer.

A conflict is useful knowledge with uncertainty. Group exact subject/property or
relation/identity candidates, preserving exact typed statements and every source,
role, Hub/source revision and observed time. Equal positions may be visually
grouped but never lose provenance. Do not coerce 7, "7" and "7 days", average
values or prefer recency, configuration, publication or human attribution as truth.

## Guidance and Questions

Show active Guidance as attributed human direction at its exact Question scope
and revision. Needs Review is contested; Open/Needs Review shows needed action.
Resolved means no pending maintainer action at that revision, not settled truth.
Conflicts without a Question are untracked; missing-evidence Questions require
no placeholder position. Broad decisions belong in Domain/System knowledge.

## Snapshot-default and current source

Keep relevant Published positions first. Add current source only when explicitly
requested or required; it is transient, with separate authority/provenance.
Age/conflict does not trigger a probe. Unavailable source leaves snapshots and
degradation reasons intact; differing current evidence adds a position, not an
update. Omitted counts disclose bounds without hiding a preferred competing claim.

## Failure boundaries

Label broken provenance without promoting siblings to truth. Unsafe current
values are redacted per position. Question caches rebuild from Hub documents and
have no authority. Prior corrected bytes remain in Git history; Draft stays in
inspection, not ordinary query. No answer resolves a Question, publishes Guidance
or writes back. [Question lifecycle](../07-conflicts-and-questions/02-question-lifecycle.md)
and [runtime requirements](07-runtime-requirements.md) own the exact contracts.
