this index establishes Capability 04 as their behavioral route without copying
the definitions.

  [`AB-SCHEMA-030..040`, `AB-SCHEMA-042..051`](../05-knowledge-entry/06-runtime-requirements.md#concrete-schema-catalog).
- Provider-neutral Resource eligibility and coverage:
  [`AB-SCHEMA-052..055`](../05-knowledge-entry/06-runtime-requirements.md#concrete-schema-catalog).
- Compact type-neutral placement and standalone reading boundary:
  [`AB-COMPACT-004..010`](../02-hub-domain-repository-model/10-compact-profile-layout-requirements.md#requirements).

valid OKF types remain readable but are not silently rewritten.
